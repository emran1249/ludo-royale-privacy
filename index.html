Privacy Policy

App: Ludo Royale Kingdom (Android package com.emran1249.ludoroyale) · Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the Ludo Royale Kingdom mobile game ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is an offline board game. You do not need an account to play.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your nickname, settings (sound, music, vibration, volume, board theme), coins, level, game history and statistics are saved locally on your phone. We do not receive or store this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

2. Advertising

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad views, ad taps and diagnostic information such as crash logs.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

3. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

4. Security

All network traffic made by the advertising SDK uses encrypted HTTPS connections. The game itself does not send your game data anywhere.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app. For questions about data processed by AdMob, or any privacy request, email us at the address below.

6. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when new features such as online play are added. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
