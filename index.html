Privacy Policy

App: Ludo Royale Kingdom (Android package com.emran1249.ludoroyale) · Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how the Ludo Royale Kingdom mobile game ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is a board game that can be played offline or online with friends. You do not need an account to play.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your nickname, settings (sound, music, vibration, volume, board theme), coins, level, game history and statistics are saved locally on your phone. We do not receive or store this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

2. Online play (optional)

If you choose Play Online (Find Players or a private room), the app uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database, servers in Singapore) to run game rooms. Firebase gives your phone an anonymous random user ID. The room stores your nickname, seat colour, online status and the game moves. Other players in the same room, including strangers matched by Find Players, can see your nickname. There is no chat. Please do not use your real name as a nickname if you do not want others to see it. We do not collect your email, phone number, contacts or real name. A room is deleted automatically when every player has left it, and any abandoned room is deleted after at most a few hours.

3. Game statistics (Firebase Analytics)

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics. It records events such as which screens are opened, when a game starts or ends, the game mode and result, together with a random app-instance ID, device model, Android version, app version and approximate location (country/city, derived from the IP address). It does not collect your advertising ID, name, email or contacts, and this data is not used for advertising. Google processes it for us as a service provider; see Firebase privacy.

4. Advertising

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad views, ad taps and diagnostic information such as crash logs.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has entered information in an online room, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

All network traffic (Firebase and AdMob) uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Offline games are never sent anywhere. Only players with the room code can join a room.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can delete all game data on your phone by uninstalling the app. To delete any online room data or analytics data linked to your device, or to ask any privacy question, email us at the address below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when new features are added. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
